# Objectives Comparison

**Selected Project Area:** Misinformation on Social Media — Social Platform / Trust and Safety

| **Category** | **VISION A — IMPROVING COVERAGE** | **VISION B — IMPROVING RESPONSE TIME** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vision** | A Community Notes Product Manager at X must decide which groups of posts or note requests need targeted note-writing effort to reduce gaps in Community Notes coverage.<br><br>**Plain-language focus:** Which posts or note requests need more note-writing effort? | A Community Notes Product Manager at X must decide which types of notes need additional rating effort to reduce the time required to reach a final status.<br><br>**Plain-language focus:** Which existing notes need more ratings so they can reach a clear status faster? |
| **Sourced Facts**<br><br>*Verified through official sources* | • Community Notes contributors can write notes and rate notes written by others.<br>• X publishes data on notes, ratings, note-status history, contributor enrollment, and note requests.<br>• The public-data schema does not document the full contributor funnel, such as request impressions, opens, writing attempts, or abandoned drafts. | • A note is shown when it is rated Helpful by enough contributors who have disagreed in some past ratings.<br>• Cross-perspective agreement is intended to reduce one-sided ratings.<br>• Note status is determined by the scoring system rather than rating count alone.<br>• The public-data schema documents submitted ratings but not note impressions, opens, review attempts, or abandoned ratings. |
| **Desired Outcomes** | • Increase the share of feed-eligible requests receiving a contributor-written note within a consistent observation window.<br>• Reduce eligible requests that remain uncovered after sufficient observation time.<br>• Identify and reduce persistent, data-definable group coverage gaps.<br>• Improve coverage without encouraging rushed or poorly supported notes. | • Increase the share of notes reaching `Helpful` and public display within a consistent observation window.<br>• Reduce time from note creation to first `Helpful` for notes that become visible.<br>• Reduce potentially resolvable notes remaining in `Needs More Ratings` because of insufficient rating attention.<br>• Improve time to display without weakening broad agreement or increasing later status reversals. |
| **Measurement Definition** | **Start:** request-feed eligibility timestamp.<br>**Event:** first new note created after eligibility.<br>Requests without a new note by the dataset cutoff remain uncovered observations.<br>The observation window must be selected from the data or confirmed with a stakeholder. | **Provisional start:** note creation.<br>**Provisional event:** first `Helpful` status, when the note becomes eligible for public display.<br>Notes not reaching `Helpful` by the dataset cutoff remain unresolved observations; their waiting time is not a completed time to Helpful.<br>This is narrower than “final status”; the official endpoint requires stakeholder confirmation. |
| **Risks** | • **Stale or incorrect targeting:** historical gaps may no longer require attention.<br>• **Attention displacement:** improving one group may reduce coverage elsewhere.<br>• **Coverage without quality:** more notes may not produce more helpful context.<br>• **Incorrect diagnosis:** low coverage may result from limited sources, knowledge, language fit, or capability rather than low visibility. | • **Incorrect diagnosis:** unresolved notes may reflect disagreement or weak quality rather than insufficient ratings.<br>• **Speed over agreement:** faster display could weaken cross-perspective agreement.<br>• **Unstable outcomes:** notes may reach Helpful and later lose the status.<br>• **Selection bias:** measuring only successful notes would hide unresolved and Not Helpful notes. |
| **Tradeoffs** | • Coverage quantity vs. note quality.<br>• Group-level gap reduction vs. overall platform coverage.<br>• Reallocating existing writers vs. recruiting new capacity.<br>• Recent responsiveness vs. stable long-term estimates.<br>• Targeted matching vs. contributor autonomy and engagement. | • Faster display vs. confidence and status stability.<br>• Older unresolved backlog vs. newly created, timely notes.<br>• Spreading ratings broadly vs. concentrating ratings to reach outcomes faster.<br>• Efficiently prioritizing near-threshold notes vs. continuing to serve difficult cases. |
| **Constraints** | • Writer capacity is limited and variable; the team cannot assume contributors will respond to prioritization.<br>• Contributors may lack the required language, local context, subject knowledge, or source access.<br>• Public data cannot fully explain why contributors did not write a note.<br>• Available fields may not support every meaningful request-group definition.<br>• Published documentation does not fully explain request-feed eligibility rules. | • Rater capacity is limited and variable.<br>• More ratings alone may be insufficient; the scoring system also requires an appropriate mix of perspectives.<br>• Public data cannot fully explain why contributors did not submit ratings.<br>• The scoring system prevents inferring exactly how many additional ratings a note needs.<br>• Recently created notes have incomplete observation time. |
| **Evidence Needed — Materiality / Unresolved Population** | Determine whether the gap is large enough to justify action using eligible-request counts, covered and uncovered counts, coverage rates, and group sample sizes. | Measure status distribution, time to first Helpful, unresolved waiting time, long-wait `Needs More Ratings` notes, and sample sizes by observable note type. |
| **Evidence Needed — Persistence / Cause** | Test whether the gap still exists and may recur using weekly or monthly coverage, request volume, uncovered counts, recent-versus-historical results, and a consistent observation window. | Distinguish insufficient attention from disagreement or poor quality using rating counts, arrival rates, time since latest rating, rating patterns, perspective agreement, and available quality indicators. |
| **Evidence Needed — Actionability / Intervention Effect** | Examine eligibility-to-first-note time, active writers, note volume, later note status, and possible capacity or knowledge-fit limitations. Validate interventions through a pilot comparing prompts, matching, recruitment, or reallocation. | Use a pilot comparing prioritized and non-prioritized notes. Examine time to first Helpful, Helpful rate, later reversals, effects on non-targeted notes, and rater participation. |
| **Measures of Success** | **Primary:** Eligible Request Coverage Rate within a consistent observation window.<br>**Supporting:** Uncovered Request Rate; Coverage Gap Between Groups.<br>Coverage must be measured on unique feed-eligible requested posts receiving at least one new note after eligibility. | **Primary:** Helpful Display Rate within a consistent observation window.<br>**Supporting:** Median Time to First Helpful; Long-Wait Unresolved Rate.<br>**Guardrail:** Status Reversal Rate.<br>Time to Helpful must be reported with the display rate so unresolved notes are not hidden. |
| **Questions Requiring a Real Stakeholder** | • What makes a request eligible for the contributor feed?<br>• What size and duration of a gap justify intervention?<br>• Which request groups are meaningful and actionable?<br>• Are internal request impressions, opens, writing attempts, and abandoned drafts available?<br>• Can X redirect writers, recruit expertise, improve matching, or combine these approaches? | • Does “final status” mean first non-NMR, first Helpful, locked status, or another event?<br>• What is an acceptable time-to-display target?<br>• How does X distinguish insufficient ratings from disagreement or poor quality?<br>• Which note types should receive priority?<br>• What level of reversal, rating displacement, or rater fatigue is acceptable? |
| **Main Decision and Uncertainty** | **Decision:** where additional note-writing effort should go.<br>**Main uncertainty:** eligibility rules and reliable, actionable group definitions.<br>**Main risk:** directing writers toward an incorrect or outdated gap. | **Decision:** where additional rating effort should go.<br>**Main uncertainty:** the correct endpoint and the cause of unresolved notes.<br>**Main risk:** accelerating unstable notes or notes lacking broad agreement. |
